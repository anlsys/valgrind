CONTEXT
With the ever growing intra-node parallelism in supercomputers, porting simulation codes while ensuring parallel execution correctness is challenging.
\emph{Determinacy races} are concurrent programming hazards, when two accesses on the memory address are not ordered and at least one is writing~\cite{determinator}.
The presence of determinacy races often hints correctness issues, as its output becomes non-deterministic given the same input.
